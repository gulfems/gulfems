### 🧠 Research

My MA thesis was the first comprehensive study of how Turkish-speaking children acquire prosodic focus and cleft sentences: three experiments with 3–6-year-olds, all materials made by hand. I'm interested in language acquisition, the syntax–prosody interface, and what language models can tell us about how children learn.

📄 Moorcroft, G., & Nakipoğlu, M. (2025). Acquisition of prosodic focus and cleft sentences. *Dilbilim Araştırmaları Dergisi*, 36(2), 225–262.
